PROPERTY INFORMATION

Property Legal Status

Before purchasing a property, buyers should carefully check ownership documents, relevant approvals and other applicable legal requirements.

  • Verify ownership documents.
  • Check the relevant authority's approvals, where applicable.
  • Confirm the property's location and identity.
  • Check any outstanding dues or restrictions.
  • Consult a qualified property lawyer when necessary.

Legal status must be checked separately for each property. No property should be described as legally verified without supporting verification.
